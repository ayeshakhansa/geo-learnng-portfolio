# Project 7: GEO Content Measurement and Portfolio Report

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates how I documented and reported content improvements made during a basic GEO audit for a tuition academy.

The report compares the original content with the recommended improvements and evaluates changes in:

* Clarity
* Structure
* Relevance
* User intent coverage
* Trustworthiness

This project focuses on **documented content-quality improvements**. It does not claim measured increases in website traffic, search rankings, conversions, or inclusion in AI-generated answers.

## Business Scenario

**Business Type:** Tuition Academy

**Target Audience:** Students and parents looking for clear and reliable information about tuition classes and academic support.

## Original Content

> “Our tuition academy is the best academy for students. Our teachers are very good and help students get high marks. We offer all subjects and provide the best education. Join our academy today because your success is guaranteed.”

## Measurement Baseline

Before recommending improvements, the original content was evaluated across five areas:

| Area                 | Initial Status |
| -------------------- | -------------- |
| Clarity              | Weak           |
| Structure            | Weak           |
| Relevance            | Limited        |
| User Intent Coverage | Limited        |
| Trustworthiness      | Weak           |

### Why the Baseline Was Weak

The original content:

* Used vague and general language.
* Did not clearly explain the academy's services.
* Did not answer important questions from students and parents.
* Did not use headings or organized sections.
* Did not provide enough specific and relevant information.
* Included an unrealistic guarantee about student success.

---

## Improvements Recommended

The content was reviewed, and recommendations were made to add specific information about:

* Subjects offered
* Grade levels
* Class timings
* Location
* Fees
* Teaching approach
* Teachers' experience
* Student support

Additional recommendations included:

* Using clear and specific headings.
* Organizing information into logical sections.
* Adding FAQs based on common student and parent questions.
* Replacing vague promotional statements with useful information.
* Removing unsupported guarantee claims.

---

## Before → Improvement → Content Result

| Area            | Before                         | Improvement                                      | Documented Content Result                   |
| --------------- | ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------- |
| Clarity         | General and vague information  | Add specific service and academic details        | Information becomes easier to understand    |
| Structure       | No clear sections or headings  | Add organized sections and headings              | Content becomes easier to scan              |
| Relevance       | Limited practical information  | Add information relevant to students and parents | Better alignment with audience needs        |
| User Intent     | Important questions unanswered | Add practical details and FAQs                   | More common information needs are addressed |
| Trustworthiness | Includes a guarantee claim     | Remove unsupported claims                        | Content becomes more balanced and credible  |

The following scorecard documents the expected change in content quality based on the recommended improvements:

| -------------------- | ------- | -------------------- |
| Clarity              | Weak    | Improved             |

These ratings represent a **qualitative content assessment**, not analytics or performance data.

## Professional Client Summary

After reviewing the content, I identified several opportunities to make the tuition academy's information clearer and more useful for students and parents.

I recommended adding practical details about subjects, grade levels, timings, location, fees, teaching methods, and available student support. I also recommended organizing the content with clear headings and adding FAQs based on common audience questions.

Finally, I recommended removing the guarantee claim and replacing vague promotional language with accurate and specific information about the academy's services and approach.

## Measurement Approach

The content improvements were documented through a before-and-after qualitative comparison based on:

1. **Clarity** — Is the business information specific and easy to understand?
2. **Structure** — Is the information logically organized and easy to scan?
3. **Relevance** — Does the content provide useful information for the target audience?
4. **User Intent Coverage** — Are important audience questions answered?
5. **Trustworthiness** — Are claims realistic, accurate, and supportable?

## Reporting Limitations

This portfolio project does not measure:

* Website traffic
* Search rankings
* Conversion rates
* AI-generated answer inclusion
* Revenue or business growth

Those results would require access to real business data and performance tools over time.

The results documented in this report are limited to **observable improvements in content quality and information coverage**.
