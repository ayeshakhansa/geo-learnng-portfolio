# Project 7: GEO Measurement and Portfolio Report

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates how I measured and reported improvements made during a basic GEO audit for a tuition academy. The report documents the content problems, improvements, and results.

## Business

Tuition Academy

## Target Audience

Students and parents looking for information about tuition classes.

## Original Content

"Our tuition academy is the best academy for students. Our teachers are very good and help students get high marks. We offer all subjects and provide the best education. Join our academy today because your success is guaranteed."

## GEO Audit Findings

The original content had several weaknesses:

- It was too general.
- It did not answer important questions from students and parents.
- It had no clear headings or organized sections.
-  It did not provide enough specific and relevant information.
- It included an unrealistic guarantee about students' success.

## Improvements Made

I recommended adding specific information about:

- Class timings
- Location
- Fees
- Subjects
- Teaching approach
- Teachers' experience
- Student support

I also recommended using clear headings and replacing the guarantee claim with realistic and supported information.

## Before → Improvement → Result

| Area | Before | Improvement | Result |
|------|--------|-------------|--------|
| Clarity | Content was too general. | Added specific information. | Content became more informative and easier to understand. |
| Structure | Content had no clear headings. | Added clear headings and organized sections. | Content became clearer and easier to navigate. |
| Trustworthiness | Claimed students' success was guaranteed. | Replaced the guarantee claim with realistic information. | Content became more credible and trustworthy. |

## Professional Client Summary

After reviewing the content, I identified several areas for improvement, such as adding information about class timings, location, fees, relevant subjects, and student support. I also recommended using clear and useful headings to help students and parents understand the information more easily. Finally, I recommended replacing the guarantee claim with realistic information about the teachers' experience, teaching approach, and student support.

## Measurement Approach

The improvements were measured by comparing the original content with the recommended version based on:

- Clarity
- Structure
- Relevance
- User intent
- Trustworthiness

This report focuses on documented content improvements and does not claim guaranteed visibility or inclusion in AI-generated answers.
