# Project 1: How GEO Can Help Small Businesses
## Project Overview
This project demonstrates how Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) can help small businesses create clear, useful, and trustworthy online content.
## Target Audience
The target audience is mainly small business owners who want to understand how GEO can support their online content and visibility.
## User Questions
- How can GEO help my business?
- Why is GEO useful for my business?
- Can GEO help my business grow?
## User Intent
The user intent is informational because small business owners want to learn how GEO can help their businesses.
## Answer-First Content
Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) can help small businesses improve the clarity, usefulness, and visibility of their online content. GEO encourages businesses to create clear, useful, and trustworthy content that users and AI-powered search systems can understand more easily and potentially use in generated answers. One important benefit of GEO is that customers may find useful information more easily, while AI systems can better understand the business's content. For example, a small hotel can create clear and useful pages about its rooms, prices, facilities, booking process, and services.
