# Project 6: Basic GEO Audit Template for Small Businesses

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates a reusable basic GEO audit template for reviewing small business website content.

The template evaluates content across five areas:

1. Clarity
2. User Intent
3. Structure
4. Relevance
5. Trustworthiness

A tuition academy is used as a practical example to demonstrate how the template can identify content weaknesses and produce actionable recommendations.

## Business

**Business Type:** Tuition Academy

## Target Audience

Students and parents looking for clear and reliable information about tuition classes and academic support.

## Content Audited

> “We are the best tuition academy for students. Our teachers are very good and help students get high marks. We offer all subjects and provide the best education. Join our academy today because your success is guaranteed.”

## Quick Audit Summary

| Audit Area      | Status            | Main Issue                                            |
| --------------- | ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Clarity         | Needs improvement | Services and academic details are too general         |
| User Intent     | Needs improvement | Important parent and student questions are unanswered |
| Structure       | Needs improvement | Information is presented as one unorganized paragraph |
| Relevance       | Needs improvement | Limited practical information for the target audience |
| Trustworthiness | Needs improvement | Includes unsupported and unrealistic claims           |

---

# Completed GEO Audit

## 1. Clarity

**Finding:**
The content is too general and does not clearly explain the academy's subjects, class levels, or academic services.

**Recommendation:**
Add specific information about subjects, grade levels, teaching methods, and available student support.

**Example questions to answer:**

* Which subjects are offered?
* Which grade levels are supported?
* What type of academic support is available?

---

## 2. User Intent

**Finding:**
The content does not answer important questions that students and parents may have before choosing a tuition academy.

**Missing information may include:**

* Class timings
* Location
* Fees
* Teaching approach
* Subjects offered
* Grade levels
* Student support

**Recommendation:**
Organize the website content around the information students and parents need to make an informed decision.

---

## 3. Structure

**Finding:**
The content does not use clear headings or organized sections.

**Recommendation:**
Use sections such as:

* About the Academy
* Subjects and Classes
* Teaching Approach
* Fees and Timings
* Frequently Asked Questions
* Contact Information

This structure can make important information easier to scan and understand.

---

## 4. Relevance

**Finding:**
The content does not provide enough practical information relevant to students and parents.

**Recommendation:**
Include useful details such as:

* Subjects
* Grade levels
* Teaching approach
* Fees
* Timings
* Location
* Academic support

Only include information that is accurate and available for the business.

---

## 5. Trustworthiness

**Finding:**
The claims **“We are the best”**, **“provide the best education”**, and **“your success is guaranteed”** are vague, subjective, or unrealistic.

**Recommendation:**
Replace unsupported claims with specific and verifiable information about the academy's services, teachers, qualifications, teaching approach, or facilities.

For example, instead of promising guaranteed success, explain what academic support the academy provides.

---

# Professional Client Recommendation

Based on this GEO audit, I recommend replacing vague promotional language with clear and specific information about the academy's services.

Priority improvements should include:

1. Clearly listing subjects and grade levels.
2. Adding information about fees, timings, and location where appropriate.
3. Explaining the teaching approach and available student support.
4. Organizing the content with clear headings.
5. Adding FAQs based on common student and parent questions.
6. Removing unsupported claims and replacing them with accurate information.

# Reusable GEO Audit Checklist

## Clarity

* [ ] Is the business or service clearly explained?
* [ ] Is the language specific and easy to understand?
* [ ] Are vague statements identified?

## User Intent

* [ ] Does the content answer important user questions?
* [ ] Is important decision-making information available?
* [ ] Are information gaps identified?

## Structure

* [ ] Does the content use clear headings?
* [ ] Is information organized logically?
* [ ] Is the content easy to scan?

## Relevance

* [ ] Does the content match the target audience's needs?
* [ ] Does it provide useful and relevant information?
* [ ] Are practical details included where appropriate?

## Trustworthiness

* [ ] Are major claims accurate and supportable?
* [ ] Are guarantees and exaggerated claims avoided?
* [ ] Is important business information kept accurate and up to date?

## Recommendations

* [ ] Are the recommendations specific?
* [ ] Are they practical for the business?
* [ ] Are priority improvements clearly identified?
