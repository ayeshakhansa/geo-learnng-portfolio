# Project 6: Basic GEO Audit Template

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates a basic GEO audit for a tuition academy. The audit identifies problems related to clarity, user intent, structure, relevance, and trustworthiness and provides practical recommendations for improvement.

## Business

Tuition Academy

## Target Audience

Students and parents looking for reliable information about tuition classes.

---

## Content Audited

"We are the best tuition academy for students. Our teachers are very good and help students get high marks. We offer all subjects and provide the best education. Join our academy today because your success is guaranteed."

---
---

## GEO Audit

### 1. Clarity

**Finding:**  
The content is too general and does not clearly explain the academy's subjects, classes, or services.

**Recommendation:**  
Add specific information about subjects, class levels, teaching methods, and student support.

### 2. User Intent

**Finding:**  
The content does not answer important questions about class timings, location, teaching approach, and student support.

**Recommendation:**  
Add clear information about these details so students and parents can easily find the information they need.

### 3. Structure

**Finding:**  
The content does not use clear headings or organized sections to present the information logically.

**Recommendation:**  
Add clear headings and organized sections to make the content easier to read and navigate.

### 4. Relevance

**Finding:**  
The content does not provide enough information that is relevant to the needs of students and parents.

**Recommendation:**  
Add useful information about subjects, class levels, fees, timings, location, and student support.

### 5. Trustworthiness

**Finding:**  
The claim that students' success is guaranteed is unrealistic and unsupported.

**Recommendation:**  
Replace the guarantee claim with realistic and supported information about the teachers' experience, teaching approach, and student support.

---

## Professional Client Recommendation
Based on my GEO audit, I recommend adding specific information about class timings, location, fees, and student support. I also recommend replacing the guarantee claim with realistic and supported information about the teachers' experience, teaching approach, and student support.
